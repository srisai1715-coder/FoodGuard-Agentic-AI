# FDA Food Additives and E-Numbers Reference Guide

## Overview of Food Additives
Food additives are substances added to food to preserve flavor, enhance taste, improve appearance, or extend shelf life. In the U.S., additives are regulated by the FDA under the Food Additive Amendments of 1958 or designated GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe). In Europe, additives are coded using E-numbers.

## Major Functional Classes of Additives

### 1. Preservatives
- **BHA (Butylated Hydroxyanisole) & BHT (Butylated Hydroxytoluene) / E320, E321:** Antioxidants used to prevent fats and oils from becoming rancid. Classified as GRAS by FDA, but subject to consumer scrutiny due to ongoing safety studies.
- **Sodium Benzoate / E211:** Inhibits mold and bacterial growth in acidic foods like soft drinks and fruit juices. When combined with Vitamin C (ascorbic acid), trace amounts of benzene may form.
- **Sodium Nitrite & Sodium Nitrate / E250, E251:** Preservatives used in cured meats (bacon, hot dogs, ham) to prevent botulism and maintain pink color. Can form nitrosamines during high-heat cooking.

### 2. Emulsifiers and Stabilizers
- **Soy Lecithin / E322:** Natural emulsifier derived from soybeans, keeps water and fat blended in chocolates and baked goods. Contains soy allergen proteins unless highly refined.
- **Mono- and Diglycerides / E471:** Emulsifiers derived from plant or animal fats used in baked goods to improve texture and shelf life. May contain small amounts of trans fats.
- **Xanthan Gum / E415 & Guar Gum / E412:** Polysaccharide thickeners and stabilizers used in gluten-free foods, sauces, and ice creams.

### 3. Artificial Sweeteners
- **Aspartame / E951:** Intense low-calorie sweetener (200x sweeter than sugar). Contains phenylalanine; products must carry a warning for individuals with phenylketonuria (PKU).
- **Sucralose / E955:** Zero-calorie sweetener derived from sugar (600x sweeter than sugar), stable under heat.
- **Acesulfame Potassium (Ace-K) / E950:** Often blended with sucralose or aspartame to mask bitter aftertastes.

### 4. Food Coloring & Dyes
- **FD&C Red No. 40 (Allura Red / E129), Yellow No. 5 (Tartrazine / E102), Yellow No. 6 (Sunset Yellow / E110):** Synthetic petroleum-derived dyes. Yellow 5 requires explicit label disclosure because it can cause allergic-type reactions in sensitive individuals.
